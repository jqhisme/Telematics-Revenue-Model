Telematics Pricing Model (Annual)

Explore the financial impact of telematics adoption on insurers and insureds. All calculations are performed on an annual basis.

Overall Assumptions:

  • The telematics discount rate is constant across all policy holders, while in the real world it may vary based on individual driving behavior and risk profiles.
  • Customer acquisition costs are calculated using the current year's telematics adoption rate, which understates the future retention benefits of telematics and may therefore overestimate acquisition costs in subsequent years.
  • Societal benefits are estimated under an ideal model assuming telematics fully reduces negative driving behaviors. Figures such as lives saved, injuries prevented, fuel savings, and CO₂ reductions should be interpreted as upper-bound estimates rather than guaranteed outcomes.
  • The model does not account for potential changes in regulatory environment or competitive landscape that may arise from increased telematics adoption.
